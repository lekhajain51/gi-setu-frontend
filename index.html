Why GI Setu

Heritage deserves to be seen, understood and valued.

GI Setu is envisioned as a bridge between India's distinctive regional products and the people who want to learn about them. It brings origin stories, traditional knowledge and maker profiles into a clear, approachable experience.

Our goal is to make discovery easier while respecting the communities and traditions that give each product its identity.
